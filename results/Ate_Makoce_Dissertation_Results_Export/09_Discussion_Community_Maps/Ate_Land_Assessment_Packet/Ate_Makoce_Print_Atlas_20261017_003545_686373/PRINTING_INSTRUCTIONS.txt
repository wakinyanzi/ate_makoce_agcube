ATE MAKOCE — PRINTING INSTRUCTIONS

Page size: US Letter, landscape (11 x 8.5 inches)
Pages: 26
Print: Actual Size / 100%, not Fit to Page
Duplex: Flip on SHORT edge for landscape pages
Binding: Spiral, comb, binder, or Japanese stab binding
Margins: Mirrored with extra space at the inner edge
Paper: 28-32 lb matte text; heavier cardstock for covers
Print a two-sided test sheet before printing the full book.
The PDF is in reading order, NOT folded-signature order.
For saddle stitching, booklet imposition and a page count divisible by four are required.
Review original map titles, photo credits, resolution, and sharing permissions before distribution.
